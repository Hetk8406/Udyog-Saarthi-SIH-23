1. **Homepage:**
   - An introductory section explaining the app's purpose and benefits.
   - Clear navigation options to key sections of the app.
   - A welcoming message or image to create a positive first impression.

2. **User Profiles:**
   - A section for users to create or update their profiles, including personal information, disability type, assistive devices used, and any specific needs or requirements.
   - Option to upload a photo for identification purposes.

3. **Job Listings:**
   - A comprehensive list of job opportunities available under the 4% reservation in government and PSU jobs.
   - Filters and search options to help users find relevant job openings.
   - Clear job descriptions, including eligibility criteria and application deadlines.

4. **Private Sector Opportunities:**
   - Information about equal job opportunities in the private sector.
   - Details on companies or organizations known for inclusivity and job openings for persons with disabilities.

5. **Notifications:**
   - Alerts and notifications about important dates related to job applications, examination schedules, and interview dates.
   - Option to customize notification preferences.

6. **Learning Resources:**
   - Support for model question papers, practice exams, and mock tests to help users prepare for qualifying examinations.
   - Educational content to enhance skills and knowledge.

7. **Application Tracking:**
   - A dashboard or section where users can track their job applications, examination attendance, qualification for interviews, and placement status.

8. **Accessibility Features:**
   - Tools and options for users to customize the app's accessibility settings, such as text size, contrast, and voice-guided navigation.

9. **Assistive Technology Integration:**
   - Integration of assistive technologies that cater to specific disabilities, such as voice commands or screen readers.

10. **Support and Assistance:**
    - Contact information or chat support for users to seek assistance or ask questions.
    - Guidance on how to use the app effectively.

11. **Success Stories:**
    - Real-life success stories of individuals with disabilities who have found employment through the app to inspire users.

12. **User Feedback:**
    - A feedback mechanism to gather input from users about their experiences and suggestions for improvements.

13. **Privacy and Security:**
    - Clear information about data privacy and security measures to reassure users of their information's protection.

14. **Legal Information:**
    - Links to legal documents such as privacy policies and terms of use.

15. **Support for Parents and Job Coaches:**
    - Resources and information for parents and job coaches to assist users in navigating the app and the job application process.

16. **Training and Skill Development Programs:**
    - Notifications about training programs offered by institutions like NIEPMD to help users enhance their skills and qualifications.

17. **News and Updates:**
    - A section for news related to job opportunities, policy changes, and updates within the disability employment sector.

18. **Logout and Profile Management:**
    - An option for users to log out securely and manage their profiles.
